# China Cross-Border E-Commerce Growth and Operations Dashboard
An Excel dynamic dashboard analyzing 7001 rows of a China cross-border e-commerce enterprise sales data from 2019 to 2025. 
## Executive Summary
This project dives deep into the development of an interactive growth and operations dashboard analyzing ¥88.4M in cross-border transaction volume across 139,193 annual orders, identification of four bottlenecks that hinders the company's growth, and proposed solutions to address the company's systemic issues.

## Key Insights
* **Key Revenue and Profit Drivers:** High-value customer segment brought in ¥45M in total revenue despite making only 41% (57,145) of the total orders.
* **Demographics:** The primary audience of the company are surprisingly young within the age bracket of 25 to 34 years old.
* **Marketing Funnel Performance:** The user experience conversion funnel revealed that the audience were mainly driven into the page through affiliate channels and app push campaigns driving 793,356 monthly page views and 205,575 monthly cart additions.
## Tools and Concepts Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning and standardization, data modeling, pivot tables, dynamic slicers and dashboard
* **Data Visualization:** Executive KPI cards along with other financial and customer values, marketing and funnel performance, and operation, logisics, and customer support.
